Caitlin Ella

Caitlin Ella is a 21-year-old singer-songwriter whose music blends vulnerability with a rich classical foundation. A passionate musician from a young age, Caitlin was classically trained in harp and violin, developing a deep appreciation for harmonic complexity and emotive expression. As a teenager, she began channelling her personal experiences into songwriting, crafting sounds that explore themes of love, loss, and self-discovery. Her music fuses jazz, soul, and classical influences, creating a unique sound that is both deeply emotional and harmonically intricate. Caitlin draws inspiration from artists like Adele, Jorja Smith, Raye, and Hannah Rowe, while maintaining a style all her own. With each song, she invites listeners into her world – offering both a cathartic release and a soulful journey of healing.
